[360] In order to characterize its essential quality, Lao-tzu likens it to water:

The highest good is like that of water. The goodness of water is that it benefits the ten thousand creatures; yet itself does not scramble, but is content with the [low] places that all men disdain. It is this that makes water so near to the Way.

The idea of a “potential” could not be better expressed.

He that is without desire sees its essence,

He that clings to desire sees only its outward form.
