[320] The new life-giving symbol springs from Prometheus’ love for his soul-mistress, a daemonic figure indeed. One can therefore be certain that, interwoven with the new symbol and its living beauty, there will also be the element of evil, for otherwise it would lack the glow of life as well as beauty, since life and beauty are by nature morally neutral. That is why the Epimethean, collective mentality finds nothing estimable in it. It is completely blinded by its one-sided moral standpoint, which is identical with the “little lamb.” The raging of Epimetheus when he turns against the “little lamb” is merely “Écrasez l’infâme” in new form, a revolt against established Christianity, which was incapable of understanding the new symbol and so giving life a new direction.
