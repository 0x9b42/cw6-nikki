Mater amabilis

Lovable Mother

Mater admirabilis

Wonderful Mother

Mater boni consilii

Mother of good counsel

Speculum justitiae

Mirror of justice

Sedes sapientiae

Seat of wisdom

Causa nostrae laetitiae

Cause of our gladness

Vas spirituale

Vessel of the spirit

Vas honorabile

Vessel of honour

Vas insigne devotionis

Noble vessel of devotion

Rosa mystica

Mystical rose

Turris Davidica

Tower of David

Turris eburnea

Tower of ivory

Domus aurea

House of gold

Foederis arca

Ark of the covenant

Janua coeli

Gate of heaven

Stella matutina

Morning star
