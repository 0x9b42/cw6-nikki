[276]     I have no wish to declare at the outset that Prometheus, the “forethinker,” stands for the introvert, and Epimetheus, the man of action and “afterthinker,” for the extravert. The conflict between these two figures is essentially a struggle between the introverted and extraverted lines of development in one and the same individual, though the poet has embodied it in two independent figures and their typical destinies.
