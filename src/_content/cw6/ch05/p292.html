[292]     It would seem that, with Spitteler, the Promethean creativity falls to the soul, while Prometheus himself merely suffers the pangs of the creative soul within him. But Goethe’s Prometheus is self-activating, he is essentially and exclusively creative, defying the gods out of the strength of his own creative power:

Who helped me

Against the pride of the Titans?

Who saved me from death?

And slavery?

Did you not do it all alone,

O ardent, holy heart?
