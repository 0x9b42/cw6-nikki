[341]     In Shatapatha Brahmana 8.1.2, 9 the role attributed to vac is a prodigious one: “Truly vac is the wise Vishvakarman, for by vac was this whole world made.” But at 1.4.5, 8–11 the question of primacy between manas and vac is decided differently:

Now it happened that Mind and Speech strove for priority one with the other. Mind said: I am better than you, for you speak nothing that I have not first discerned. Then Speech said: I am better than you, for I announce what you have discerned and make it known.

They went to Prajapati for judgment. Prajapati decided in favour of Mind, saying to Speech: Truly Mind is better than you, for you copy what Mind does and run in his tracks; moreover it is the inferior who is wont to imitate his betters.
